Kadena network report: October 2026

What happened on Kadena mainnet from September 15 to October 14, 2026: transactions, blocks and mining hashrate across all 20 chains, measured by Kadena Nexus from public chain data.

Published October 15, 2026 · Data through October 14, 2026 (UTC) · A full-month update follows in early November.

Summary

  • In late September about 99% of all Kadena transactions were automated calls to one contract, free.block-history, which keeps an on-chain record of each block's hash and time: about 8 calls per block on every chain, 436,751 a day. They fell sharply from October 4 and had stopped by October 12. That one contract explains the drop in the total, from 441,437 transactions a day in late September to 4,716 on October 14.
  • Excluding those recorder calls, Kadena carried an average of 4,261 transactions a day from October 1 to October 14, down 9% on the second half of September (4,686 a day).
  • Mining hashrate averaged 16.4 PH/s in October so far, up 14% on late September (14.4 PH/s).
  • All 20 chains kept producing blocks: about 57,501 blocks a day, an average of 30.1 seconds per block on each chain against the 30-second target.
  • Chain 2 carried 37% of October's transactions outside the recorder, the largest share of any chain.

Most of this total was one automated workload, explained next.

The block-history recorder

free.block-history is a Pact contract that stores each block's height, hash and time on its own chain, so other contracts can look them up later. Anyone can call its attest function, which records the previous block. In late September recorder accounts called it about 8 times in every block on every chain, 436,751 transactions a day, most of Kadena's daily total. The calls fell sharply from October 4 and had stopped by October 12. These are real, fee-paying transactions, but they come from one automated source, so this report shows activity with and without them.

Transactions excluding the recorder

Everything except calls to free.block-history: transfers, swaps, contract calls and the rest, per UTC day.

Busiest day: September 25 (6,508). Quietest: October 10 (3,046).

Mining hashrate

Daily average hashrate in petahashes per second (PH/s), from the difficulty of every canonical block that day. As an outside check, MiningPoolStats, which tracks the active KDA pools, showed about 16.7 PH/s for the network on October 15.

Where transactions happen

Transactions per chain from October 1 to October 14, excluding recorder calls. Activity is uneven: a few chains host the busiest contracts.

Blocks and block time

Each chain targets one block every 30 seconds, which is 57,600 blocks a day across 20 chains. In October so far the network produced 57,501 canonical blocks a day on average, so blocks arrived every 30.1 seconds per chain. Late September averaged 57,308 a day.

Nodes

The public node crawler found 40 active nodes in 12 countries in its last scan (October 12, 2026), including the Kadena Nexus node in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. Nodes are placed by IP location, so the count covers public nodes only.

Adoption Index

The Kadena Community Edition Adoption Index combines five weighted signals (active addresses, new contracts, value locked, hashrate and developer commits) into one daily number against a summer 2026 baseline of 50. Its reading for October 14, 2026 was 54. How each input is measured is published in the methodology.

Supply

KDA in circulation was 376.3 million when this report was built, according to the indexer's network summary. The maximum supply is 1 billion KDA, most of it paid to miners over time.

Data and method

  • Source: the public Chainweb GraphQL indexer (graphql.chaddex.com), queried block by block in 50-block windows on every chain. Each UTC day starts at the first block created at or after 00:00 UTC.
  • Transactions count non-coinbase transactions in canonical blocks; orphaned blocks are excluded. As a check, the October 14 total (4,716) is within 1% of the Kadena Network Monitor's own rolling 24-hour count (4,723) when this report was built.
  • Recorder calls are transactions whose code calls free.block-history.attest, found by reading the code of every transaction in every block. Other transactions are the total minus recorder calls.
  • Hashrate is the sum of each canonical block's difficulty over the day, divided by 86,400 seconds.
  • Nodes come from the public node crawler (nodes.denascan.com); Adoption Index from adoption.kadena.ws; supply from the indexer's network summary.
  • Figures are Kadena Nexus measurements from public data, not official statistics. Please cite "Kadena Nexus, Kadena network report: October 2026".
