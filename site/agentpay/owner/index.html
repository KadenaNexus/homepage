New here? The four words you need
Allowance
A budget you create for one agent. You give it a name, and only your account can change it.
Vault
The on-chain account that holds the allowance's KDA. Only you can take KDA out. The agent can only pay from it, within your limits.
Agent key
The key your agent's software uses to pay. It comes from the agent, not from your wallet. It cannot withdraw and cannot change limits. If it leaks, the most that can be spent is what your limits allow, and you can replace it.
Limits
The most the agent may pay in one payment, and the most in one day (the day follows UTC time). The chain refuses anything above them.

Stop and start

What does pausing do?

While paused, the agent can pay nothing at all. Your KDA stays in the vault and you can still withdraw it. Use it the moment something looks wrong. Resume turns payments back on with the same limits.

Money in the vault

What do these two buttons do?

Put in the vault moves KDA from your account into the vault, where the agent can spend it within your limits. Withdraw to my account moves KDA from the vault back to you. You can withdraw any amount at any time, paused or not. Start with a small amount.

Limits

How do the limits work?

Per payment is the most the agent may pay at once. Per day is the most it may pay in one UTC day in total. The daily limit cannot be lower than the per-payment limit. New limits apply to the next payment.

Agent key

When should I replace the agent key?

When you move to a new agent, or if you think the agent's key was copied. The old key stops working at once. Your KDA, limits and history stay as they are.

Who the agent may pay

What is the approved sellers list?

By default the agent may pay any account, within your limits. Turn Approved sellers only on, and it may pay only the accounts you approved. Approve adds an account to that list; Remove takes it off. A seller account must already exist on this chain.
