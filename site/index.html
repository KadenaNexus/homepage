What is Kadena (KDA)?

Kadena is a proof-of-work blockchain that runs 20 chains in parallel, a design called Chainweb, so it can handle more transactions while keeping mining security. Smart contracts are written in Pact, a readable language built for safe on-chain code. KDA is the coin used for fees and paid to miners.

Kadena FAQ

Kadena Community Edition today

Kadena LLC wound down in October 2025. Since November 8, 2025 the chain has run as Kadena Community Edition: the same chain, KDA and accounts, with node software published by former Kadena engineers and community developers. Network upgrades are switched on by miner vote.

Live Kadena network statistics

Each of the 20 chains targets a block every 30 seconds, about 40 blocks a minute across the network. The Kadena Network Monitor shows every block as it lands, throughput, confirmation times and where the nodes run. The Adoption Index publishes one daily number for how much Kadena is used.

How to buy, store and use KDA

Keep KDA in a wallet where you hold the keys, such as eckoWALLET or Linx. You can buy it on an exchange that still lists it, bridge USDC to Kadena and swap on a Kadena DEX, trade person to person in escrow, or earn it through community tasks. Crypto prices move a lot, so start small.

We put money on the escrow contract ourselves

Before the marketplace opened, the escrow contract went through several independent security reviews. We then left a 10,000 KDA bounty sitting in it, in public, for anyone who could break it. Nobody did.

The contract is non-custodial, uses account-level authorization for every debit, and changes to it need two of two governance signatures.

Kadena FAQ and current development

Short answers to what people ask most. More in the Kadena FAQ.

Is Kadena still running?

Yes. After Kadena LLC wound down, the community release of the node software took over on November 8, 2025. Miners kept mining, and the chain, KDA and accounts carried on unchanged as Kadena Community Edition.

Is Kadena quantum safe?

Not yet on mainnet. Community developers are adding q: accounts signed with SLH-DSA (SPHINCS+), a hash-based signature built to resist quantum computers. Quantum-Safe Kadena explains it and tracks progress.

Who develops Kadena now?

Former Kadena engineers and community developers publish the node software under the kda-community organization on GitHub, together with the new community DAO. Kadena Nexus works alongside them, building tools that support Kadena Community Edition.

Is Kadena Nexus the official Kadena team?

No. Kadena Nexus is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Kadena LLC. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
