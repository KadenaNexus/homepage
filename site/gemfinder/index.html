The screen

What a token has to pass

Can it be sold?

A test sale is quoted, buy and sell taxes and the cost of selling must stay under 10%, and an ordinary transfer must work.

Who controls it?

No owner power to mint, pause or block transfers, no upgradeable contract, and top holders under 50% where that can be measured.

Is the market real?

Enough liquidity and real sellers, not mostly wash trading, within an age and size range where research is useful.

"Watching" means a token passed with some checks not completed; the report names those checks. Today every pick on Ethereum and Solana is "watching". The screen does not check the team, the project or its future, and nothing it finds is proof that a token is free of risk.
