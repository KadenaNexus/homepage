Why Kadena

Kadena is a proof-of-work chain that kept running after its original company closed, and it is now maintained by its community. Each point below links to a live page, so you can check it yourself.

  • Community owned. Kadena Community Edition is developed in the open by community developers. Kadena Nexus works alongside them.
  • Low fees. See the daily fee comparison with other chains.
  • Measured, not claimed. Adoption data at the Adoption Index and public test results at Nexus Research.
  • A working way in. New users can buy KDA with USDC from Ethereum through Kadeway, approving every step in their own wallets.
  • Things to do on arrival. Solo mining on Kadena Garden, tasks on Kadena Earn, and more on the projects list.

How we work

Every Kadena Nexus partnership rests on three principles: shared risk, real activity and full transparency.

What we offer

  • A fixed payment for a clearly defined campaign or service, a revenue share on real fees, or a mix of both.
  • Profit and loss shared in proportions agreed before we start.
  • Written terms, with every payment visible on-chain.

What we do not do

  • No interest, in either direction, and no guaranteed or fixed returns on capital.
  • No leverage, margin, futures or other derivative products.
  • No wash trading, fake volume or paid price manipulation.
  • No gambling-style promotions, and no partners whose main business is gambling or interest-based lending.
  • No misleading claims about price or profit.

If a proposal needs one of these to work, it is not a fit for us, and we will say so early.
